High voltage (about 170 V DC). Unvalidated prototype. Read Safety before building. Off is not safe until you have measured it.

Tools

Two tools that work in the website (Chrome or Edge on a desktop) and in the downloadable Nixie Clock Companion app. The calculator uses the same formulas as Equations; its numbers are a model, not measurements.

Tube and HV rail calculator

Serial companion (USB to the Arduino Nano)

Safety. The Nano's USB port is inside the closed case: lift the case off the chassis to reach it. Follow USB, the shunt and the serial monitor: connect USB before 12 V, never plug or unplug it while 12 V is on, and treat the high-voltage rail as live until you have measured TP4. Connecting resets the Nano; the firmware keeps any HV fault latched across that reset.
